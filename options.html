Stretchtext

Stretchtext grows a sentence where the reader wants more and shrinks it back where they do not. Expansions are generated on demand and billed to the reader's own Pollen.

Pay with your own Pollen

The recommended path, and the one the Pollinations BYOP reference prescribes for extensions. Approve a code on enter.pollinations.ai/device and the extension receives a scoped key that works until it expires or you revoke it. No long-lived secret is pasted into the browser.

Register an App Key (optional)

The device flow works without one — leave this empty. An App Key only attributes the consent traffic to your account, so the user's spend shows up under it. Register one at enter.pollinations.ai/keys.

Paste a secret key instead

Create one at enter.pollinations.ai/keys. The key stays in this browser session and is only sent to gen.pollinations.ai.
